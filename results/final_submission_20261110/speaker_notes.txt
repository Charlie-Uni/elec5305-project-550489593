SLIDE 1
An environmental recording can contain a target and another recognisable event. My main question is how three different audio representations fail under that competition, compared with generic noise at controlled power. I use the same mixtures and explicit evaluation rules. The report distinguishes measured behaviour from a claim that semantic meaning alone causes failure.

Evidence: Project report, research question and scope.
